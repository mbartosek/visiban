Shareable group invite links can no longer grant the group `admin` role and always expire: `POST /api/v1/groups/{id}/invite-links/` refuses `role: "admin"`, an explicit `expiry_days: null` and an `expiry_days` over 30 with `400`, and an omitted `expiry_days` now defaults to 7 days instead of never expiring. The **New link** form no longer offers **Admin** or **Never**. Emailed group invites, which are bound to one address, still grant admin. Upgrading revokes unused shareable admin links created earlier and gives unused never-expiring ones a 7-day expiry (#1727, #1940).
