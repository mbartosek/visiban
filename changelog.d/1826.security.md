Analytics extension panels (`ANALYTICS_EXTENSIONS`) now declare who may see them with a `min_role` attribute (`viewer`, `collaborator`, `member`, `admin` or `site_admin`), and the board summary drops every panel above the caller's board role before calling it, so a panel that forgot its own role check can no longer show its data to viewers. **Change for extension authors:** a panel with no `min_role`, or an invalid one, is now admin-only (fail closed); set `my_panel.min_role = "viewer"` (or the lowest role that should see it) to keep showing it to other members. Deployments with no extensions registered are unaffected (#1826)
