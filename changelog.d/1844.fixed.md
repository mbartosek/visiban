The metadata row on an aging card is easier to read (#1844). On every aging tint, in light and dark themes, the row's text now meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1). That covers counts, dates, custom field names and empty-field placeholders, links and the blocked marker. Before, some of it fell well below that on the darker stages. The tint colors are unchanged, so the stages still look as distinct as before, and cards without a tint are unchanged. Filled priority badges and avatar initials still fall below 4.5:1 for some colors and are tracked in #1861. Low-contrast metadata on untinted cards is tracked in #1862.
