Card details on an aging card are easier to read (#1844). On every aging tint, in light and dark themes, the smaller text on the card now meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1). This covers counts, dates, custom field names and empty-field placeholders, links and the blocked marker. Before, it fell as low as about 2:1 on the darker stages. The tint colors are unchanged, so the stages still look as distinct as before. Cards without a tint are unchanged.
