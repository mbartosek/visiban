When another user deleted the column, swimlane, or card you had open, the card panel stayed open on a card that no longer existed. It now closes. The archived cards panel no longer lists, or offers to restore, archived cards whose column or swimlane was deleted while it was open. The WebSocket and change-feed docs now state the contract for container deletes: deleting a column, swimlane, or board deletes its cards, including archived ones, and only the one `column.deleted`, `swimlane.deleted`, or `board.deleted` event is sent, with no `card.deleted` per card. Clients should remove the container's cards when they receive it.
