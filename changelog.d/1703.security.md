Emailed site invites are now bound to the address they were sent to, matching emailed group and board invites. Visiban stores only a keyed fingerprint of the address. Creating an account from the invite requires the invited address, both for email sign-up and for OAuth; a mismatch is refused with `invite_recipient_mismatch` and the invite stays unused. Shareable site invite links and emailed site invites sent before upgrading are unchanged (#1703).
