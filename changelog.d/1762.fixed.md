MCP `list_cards` and the `board://{board_id}` snapshot no longer truncate silently at 200 cards: a plain `list_cards` call that is cut off now says so in-band (an extra text block and `_meta` `visiban/total`/`visiban/truncated`, with the array and `structuredContent` unchanged), `list_cards` gains optional `limit`/`offset` paging and an opt-in `include_meta` flag returning `{cards, total, truncated, limit, offset}`, and the snapshot gains `cards_total` and `cards_truncated` fields and is documented as capped at 200 cards (#1762)
