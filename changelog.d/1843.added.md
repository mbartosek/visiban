Stale cards that do not show the `Stale · Nd` badge now carry a small bar meter at the start of the title (#1843). This covers the Compact card layout at every density, Dense density, and cards whose badge shows an overdue or due-soon date instead. One, two or three solid bars mark 1×, 2× and 3× the staleness threshold, so the aging steps no longer differ by color alone (WCAG 1.4.1). Screen readers announce the idle days. The meter sits inside the title line and never makes a card taller. A compact card also no longer grows by an empty row when it becomes stale, due soon, or recently moved.
