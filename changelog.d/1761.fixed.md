Moving a card fails visibly and says why. When a move from the card detail panel fails, the Move popover stays open and names the cause (network error, permission, the column that hit its limit) instead of always blaming WIP or weight limits, and the card is no longer shown in the new column. Drag-and-drop and the admin "Move anyway" override now show a "Move failed" toast for failures that previously snapped the card back silently (network error, server error); a later successful move clears a stale toast; and a failed drop is no longer announced to screen readers as a success. Every bulk action now says "maintenance mode is active" rather than "something went wrong" when the instance is read-only.
