Fixed: moving a card fails visibly and says why. From the card detail panel the move no longer closes as if it succeeded: the panel stays open and names the cause (network error, permission, the column that hit its limit) instead of always blaming WIP or weight limits. Drag-and-drop now shows a toast for failures that previously snapped the card back silently (network error, server error) and no longer announces a failed move as successful to screen readers. Bulk move now says "maintenance mode is active" rather than "something went wrong" when the instance is read-only.
