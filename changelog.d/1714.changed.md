The board member list (`GET /api/v1/boards/{id}/full/` `members`) lists the board's own members (direct, group-inherited and the owner). Users with all-content access who hold no role on the board are listed only for a site-admin reader, and a board admin who is not a site admin receives `is_site_admin` on rows backed by a board membership, the rows the Members settings lock. The card assignee picker shows an assignee who is not in the member list by name.
