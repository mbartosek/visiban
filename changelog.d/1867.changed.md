`DELETE /api/v1/admin/board-invite-links/{id}/` now answers `204 No Content` (was `200` with the row) and `404` for an already-revoked invite (was `400`), matching the board-scoped revoke. The endpoint is new in 1.2 and not in any GA release; this changes its 1.2 pre-release behavior. The site invite-link revoke keeps its 1.1 behavior and is tracked in #1912.
