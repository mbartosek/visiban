Creating, sending or revoking a group invite link, creating or editing a group label, starring a group, creating a subgroup, renaming a group, changing its board defaults or a member's role, transferring its ownership, and importing a board into it no longer fail with a server error when the group is deleted at the same moment; the request now answers "not found" (an unknown-parent error for a subgroup) and writes nothing, or completes first and is then removed with the group.
