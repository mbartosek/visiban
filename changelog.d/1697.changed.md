WebSocket Origin matching no longer applies `*` or leading-dot `ALLOWED_HOSTS` entries. If your `ALLOWED_HOSTS` uses one, list your public origin (for example `https://boards.example.com`) in `CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` or `CSRF_TRUSTED_ORIGINS`, otherwise browser WebSocket connections are refused. Exact `ALLOWED_HOSTS` entries keep matching with scheme and port ignored. System check `accounts.W002` now warns about exactly this configuration (wildcard `ALLOWED_HOSTS` and no public origin listed).
