Cards in the yellow warning step of the aging tint now show the aging bar meter at the start of the title, with the shortest bar drawn as an outline and no solid bars (#1863). Before this, a warning card differed from a fresh card by color alone (WCAG 1.4.1). The meter appears on every warning card in every layout and density, never makes a card taller, and screen readers announce the idle days, for example "Idle 9 days, approaching the stale threshold".
