Card movement history is now kept when a card is hard-deleted, whether directly or by deleting its column or swimlane (#1469). The board's History view keeps listing those movements under the card's original UID and title. Deleting the whole board still removes all of its history. Summary and Analytics numbers are unchanged. Movement records are now read-only in the Django admin: they can be viewed and searched but no longer added, edited or deleted. Upgrading runs a batched backfill of `card_movements`. Multi-replica deployments that roll pods should run `manage.py backfill_card_movement_snapshots` once after the rollout. See the 1.2 upgrade notes.
