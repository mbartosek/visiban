Card movement history is now kept when a card is hard-deleted, whether directly or by deleting its column or swimlane (#1469). The board's History view keeps listing those movements under the card's original UID and title. Deleting the whole board still removes all of its history. Summary and Analytics numbers are unchanged. Movement records are now read-only in the Django admin: they can be viewed and searched but no longer added, edited or deleted. Upgrading runs a batched backfill of `card_movements`. Installs that migrate while the previous release is still serving should run `manage.py backfill_card_movement_snapshots` once after the rollout; History lists such movements either way, and the command makes them survive their card's deletion. See the 1.2 upgrade notes.
