Deleting a user from the Django admin now keeps at least one active admin on every group: deleting the last active admin of a group (or, in a bulk delete, every active admin of one) is refused with a message, as for the last active site admin. Upgrading also gives every group's owner a direct admin membership where it was missing or had a lower role, so the owner always appears as a direct admin member of their group. (#1786)
