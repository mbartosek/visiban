The card detail panel now opens next to the card's column on windows 1280 px and wider, instead of at the far right edge over a dimmed board. It opens to the right of the column if there is room, otherwise to the left, and docks at the right edge when neither side fits or the card isn't on screen. A notch on the panel points at the card, and the open card gets a thick border, a ring and `aria-current`. Click another card to switch the panel to it, or an empty part of the board to close it; focus returns to the card. Windows from 641 to 1279 px keep the right-edge panel with a lighter backdrop, and windows 640 px and narrower get a full-screen panel with a Board back button. Drag-and-drop is paused while the panel is open, the checklist, attachments and comments show placeholders while they load, and in the shared demo the panel notes that edits are visible to everyone and the pull or merge request link is read-only.
