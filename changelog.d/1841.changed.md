Card aging is now a graduated scale instead of a single stale tint (#1841). A card's tint deepens with how long it has been idle in multiples of the board's staleness threshold: yellow while approaching the threshold, orange from 1×, red from 2×, and deep red with an inset outline from 3×. The multiples are fixed. The "Stale" badge now shows the idle days (`Stale · 41d`). The tooltip, now also shown on keyboard focus, gives the idle days and the threshold multiple, and says how many cards block a stale card. Aging cards fade less than before, so the card title and badge keep WCAG AA contrast in both themes. Cards in a done column no longer get an aging tint or a "Stale" badge. Cards that have never moved, such as imported ones, age from their creation date, matching the server's stale check.
