Card aging is now a graduated scale instead of a single stale tint (#1841). A card's tint deepens with how long it has been idle in multiples of the board's staleness threshold: yellow while approaching the threshold, orange from 1×, red from 2×, and deep red with an inset outline from 3×. The multiples are fixed. The "Stale" badge now shows the idle days (`Stale · 41d`). The hover tooltip shows the idle days and the threshold multiple, and names any cards blocking a stale card. Aging cards fade less than before, so their text keeps WCAG AA contrast in both themes.
