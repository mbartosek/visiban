Partly mitigated a slowdown in reading large boards on PostgreSQL. Since the move to psycopg 3, Django 5.2 parsed every timestamp in Python and skipped psycopg's C parser. This was part of a slowdown of about 12% at the mean in `GET /api/v1/boards/{id}/full/` on a ~2,400-card board. Visiban now backports Django 6.1's fix, so timestamps are parsed in C again with unchanged responses. In local measurements this recovers roughly a third to a half of that slowdown.
