Fixed a slowdown in reading large boards on PostgreSQL. Since the move to psycopg 3, Django 5.2 parsed every timestamp in Python and skipped psycopg's C parser. On a ~2,400-card board this made `GET /api/v1/boards/{id}/full/` about 12% slower at the mean. Visiban now backports Django 6.1's fix, so timestamps are parsed in C again and responses are unchanged.
