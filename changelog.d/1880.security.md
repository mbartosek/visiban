Public board share links are now resolved by a stored SHA-256 hash of the share token, as invite links are, instead of by the token itself, and the per-token share-link rate limit keys its cache entry on that hash rather than on the canonical token. Migrations `boards/0087`–`0089` add the hash, fill it in for existing share links (which then resolve by the hash; see #1939 for links that record no enabler), and build its unique index concurrently. The share API responses are unchanged. During a rolling upgrade, a share link that a not-yet-upgraded backend pod creates, regenerates or re-enables after migration `0088` has run answers `404` until a board admin regenerates it (#1880).
