Deleting a column, swimlane or board, or deleting cards in the Django admin, now clears the blocked indicator on surviving cards that a deleted card was blocking. Each such card gets a `card.updated` WebSocket event and change-feed entry with its new `blocker_count`, as deleting a single card already did. Before, connected clients kept showing those cards as blocked until a reload. Archiving a card through the MCP `archive_card` tool, and archiving or restoring one in the Django admin, now also keep the `blocker_count` of the cards it blocks current.
