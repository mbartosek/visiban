The card detail Move popover now closes and clears its inline error when another session's change (a version-conflict board refresh or a forced move) replaces the card underneath it, and the open panel's location and version follow the refreshed card instead of a stale snapshot. If the popover was open and the card did not land where it was aiming, the panel shows "This card was changed elsewhere. Its current location is shown." The user's own saves and own move never trigger the reset or the notice. Focusing and blurring the title no longer re-sends an old title after another session renamed the card. The version-conflict toast no longer says the card was modified "while you were dragging it", since the move may have come from the detail panel (#1773).
