The card detail Move popover now closes and clears its inline error when a version-conflict board refresh or a forced move changes the card underneath it, and the open panel's location follows the refreshed card instead of a stale snapshot. If a failed move's error disappears this way, the panel shows "This card was changed elsewhere. Its current location is shown." The version-conflict toast no longer says the card was modified "while you were dragging it", since the move may have come from the detail panel (#1773).
