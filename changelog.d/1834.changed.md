OpenAPI: the `/auth/user/` components are named `CurrentUser`, `CurrentUserRequest` and `PatchedCurrentUserRequest` (1.1: `User`, `UserRequest`, `PatchedUserRequest`), and the dj-rest-auth login, registration, password-reset and password-reset-confirm responses use `Token`, `PasswordResetResponse` and `PasswordResetConfirmResponse`. The rename changes no field; clients regenerated from the schema see renamed types. The 1.1-to-1.2 mapping is in the upgrade guide (#1834).
