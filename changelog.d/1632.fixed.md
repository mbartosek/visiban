A card create or update that names a label or custom field definition a concurrent delete removes now answers 400 (unknown id) instead of 500, and the delete otherwise waits for the card write; the same holds for swimlane create with custom field values (no longer misreported as a duplicate name) and for the Django admin card form's labels, which show a form error. Custom field definition reorders now wait for in-flight card and swimlane writes instead of risking a deadlock. To make this safe, card creates and updates now wait for any in-flight change to their board's settings, labels, members, structure, stars or saved filters (including a column, swimlane or board delete, for its whole duration), and those wait for in-flight card writes; there is no timeout on that wait. Deactivating a user who owns boards while one of their card edits is in flight no longer fails with a 500.
