Changing the account email now requires the account's current password (sent as the new optional `current_password` field on `PATCH /api/v1/auth/me/` and `PATCH`/`PUT /api/v1/auth/user/`, and prompted for in Settings) for accounts that have a password; accounts that sign in only through an identity provider are not asked. Adding an address or making one primary on the `/accounts/email/` page asks for the password again first. Whenever an account's email changes, the previous address now receives a notice.
