API: every refused query parameter now answers `400` with `{detail, code}` and `code: "invalid_query_param"`. Converted outright (bodies new in 1.2): `after` and `limit` on `GET /api/v1/boards/{id}/events/`, an `offset` above 2^62 on the card list, archived cards, card timeline and board movements, `action` on `GET /api/v1/admin/action-log/`, and `status` on `GET /api/v1/admin/board-invite-links/`. Additive (bodies shipped in 1.1): a card filter value that fails its type check on `GET /api/v1/boards/{id}/cards/` (and `GET /api/v1/cards/`) keeps its field-keyed entry and gains `detail` and `code`, and an unknown `event_types` group on the card timeline gains `code`. OpenAPI: these operations declare the coded 400 schema, and `GET /api/v1/admin/action-log/` now documents its `action`, `offset` and `page_size` parameters and its paginated response (#1832).
