API: the refused query parameters below now answer `400` with `{detail, code}` and `code: "invalid_query_param"` (an invalid `moved_after`/`moved_before` on board movements is handled separately in #1833). Converted outright (these bodies appeared only in 1.2 pre-releases, never in a GA): `after` and `limit` on `GET /api/v1/boards/{id}/events/`, a malformed `cursor` on `GET /api/v1/cards/` (previously `404 {"detail": "Invalid cursor"}`, or a `500` for a decodable cursor whose position was not a timestamp), an `offset` above 2^62 on the card list, archived cards, card timeline and board movements, `action` on `GET /api/v1/admin/action-log/`, and `status` on `GET /api/v1/admin/board-invite-links/`. Additive (bodies shipped in 1.1): a card filter value that fails its type check on `GET /api/v1/boards/{id}/cards/` (and `GET /api/v1/cards/`) keeps its field-keyed entry and gains `detail` and `code`, and an unknown `event_types` group on the card timeline gains `code`. OpenAPI: these operations declare the coded 400 schema, and `GET /api/v1/admin/action-log/` now documents its `action`, `offset` and `page_size` parameters and its paginated response (#1832).
